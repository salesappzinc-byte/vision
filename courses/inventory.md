## Review gate

A lesson text change is a candidate until a person marks it.

- **Verified breaking fix:** a named official page contradicts a sentence we teach as fact. Edit that sentence. Do not republish a recording that still says the old sentence.
- **Enhancement:** a new model feature. Add a dated note. Do not rewrite the whole course.
- **New-course candidate:** a topic with no brief. Write a brief first. Do not mark it for sale.

Publishing a lesson to learners requires the owner. This file cannot publish. Creators, later, correct their own material. The platform does not auto-publish their edits.
