# Free gateway course: AI for your own work

**Status:** Written draft, 7 November 2026. Not recorded. Not piloted. Not published.

This folder is the first draft of the free beginner course described in [VISION.md](../../VISION.md) and outlined in [docs/course-research.md](../../docs/course-research.md). It is course copy for review. It is not the `/vision` skill, not a website, and not a recording.

You can take the lessons in order, at your own pace. There is no seven-day timetable. Each lesson is one sitting.

## Who it is for

You have heard of AI and want a practical start. You may be in a job, running a one-person business, making things as a creator, or doing a personal project. You do not need a technical background.

Examples follow **Sam Okonkwo**, a fictional solo bookkeeper. Sam is not a real person and not a real business. Shops in the samples are named Shop A, Shop B, and Shop C. If that is not your life, use a personal project instead. A neighbourhood library book swap is the personal alternative used in the exercises. Do not paste real client files, passwords, or private records.

## What you need

- Something to write with.
- A chat tool you are allowed to use, on this site or elsewhere. This draft does not assume a practice box exists. If the tool has a price, that price is yours. It is not a course fee. This course does not charge.
- For lesson 5, two pages you can open yourself in a browser. The lesson does not give you a web address to pretend you read.

Longer video, website, and automation lessons are **not available** in this draft. Lesson 7 only asks whether one of those topics interests you later.

## What you will hold at the end

The [Personal AI Toolkit](toolkit.md): a plain-language card, 10 reusable prompts, a one-page brief, three edited drafts, a claim-and-source log, a do-not-paste list, and a weekly task map. The [knowledge check and rubric](assessment.md) sit after lesson 7. An instructor key is in that same file, below the learner questions.
