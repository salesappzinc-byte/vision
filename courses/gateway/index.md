# Free gateway course: AI for your own work

**Status:** Written draft, desk-edited 7 November 2026. Not recorded. Not piloted. Not published. A desk edit is not a learner test.

This folder is the first draft of the free beginner course described in [VISION.md](../../VISION.md) and outlined in [docs/course-research.md](../../docs/course-research.md). It is course copy for review. It is not the `/vision` skill, not a website, and not a recording.

You can take the lessons in order, at your own pace. There is no seven-day timetable. Each lesson is one sitting.

## Who it is for

You have heard of AI and want a practical start. You may be in a job, running a one-person business, making things as a creator, or doing a personal project. You do not need a technical background.

Two fictional people appear in every lesson. **Sam Okonkwo** writes notes about three labelled practice folders, called Shop A, Shop B, and Shop C. Sam is not real. Those notes are about wording and a weekly list. They are not lessons in bookkeeping, tax, or what a shop must file. **Nia Adeyemi** is planning a neighbourhood book swap. Nia is not real. No library has agreed, and no date is set. Use Sam, Nia, or your own non-secret goal. Do not paste real client files, passwords, or private records.

## How to use a chat page

This draft does not include a chat box on the website. For any lesson that uses a tool:

1. Open your ordinary browser.
2. Open a chat page you are allowed to use. Any provider is fine. If it costs money, that cost is yours, not a course fee.
3. Paste the lesson prompt. Copy the reply into your notes.
4. Do not connect email, files, or payments. Do not press send or post.

If you have no chat page today, you can still write the lesson's artifact by hand. Say "not run yet" on it.

## What you need

- Something to write with.
- A chat tool you are allowed to use, on this site or elsewhere. This draft does not assume a practice box exists. If the tool has a price, that price is yours. It is not a course fee. This course does not charge.
- For lesson 5, a browser. One public page is named there and was opened while drafting. You still check the words on screen. You choose the second page yourself. Do not type an address you have not opened.

Longer video, website, and automation lessons are **not available** in this draft. Lesson 7 only asks whether one of those topics interests you later.

## What you will hold at the end

The [Personal AI Toolkit](toolkit.md): a plain-language card, 10 reusable prompts, a one-page brief, three edited drafts, a claim-and-source log, a do-not-paste list, and a weekly task map. The [knowledge check and rubric](assessment.md) sit after lesson 7. An instructor key is in that same file, below the learner questions.
