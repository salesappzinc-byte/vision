# Knowledge check, instructor key, and artifact rubric

**Status:** Written draft, 7 November 2026. Not recorded. Not piloted. Not published.

Learners stop at the end of the questions. The key and rubric are for a presenter or reviewer. This check is not a certificate, not a job test, and not a promise of earnings.

Course index: [index.md](index.md). Toolkit: [toolkit.md](toolkit.md).

## Learner questions

Answer in your own notes. Short answers are enough. You may look at your toolkit. Do not ask a chat tool to mark you correct.

1. Who is a chat tool for, in your case, and why might you use it? What does it do with your prompt, and what does it not know about your life?
2. Name two things a weak prompt is missing. What do you do on a second pass?
3. Why do you edit the one-page brief before you draft a post, an email, and a description?
4. The tool replies "Verified. This summary is accurate." What has to happen before you trust a claim? What will you not accept as proof?
5. Your weekly map is written. What must a person do before a message is sent, a file is changed, or money is spent? Name two kinds of information you will not paste into the tool.

When you finish, you may note an interest in a later video, website, or automation lesson, or write "not yet". That note does not enrol you. Those lessons are not for sale in this draft. If someone invites you to a community, you can say yes or no. Marketing email is a separate choice. You do not have to agree to it to finish.

## Instructor key

Do not show this section on the learner page until after they answer. Accept equivalent wording. Do not require brand names of tools.

1. **Credit** who it is for (someone who can check the words) and why (a first draft or a list, not truth). Also credit that the tool writes a likely reply and does not know the learner's life unless they pasted it. **Missing** if they say the tool knows the truth, gives tax or accounting answers, or checks the web without a page they opened.
2. **Credit** any two of: role, task, context, format, example, or an explicit ban on invented prices and quotes. The second pass must change the reply (cut, correct, or ask), not start from a blank line only. **Missing** if they only say "add more detail" and never look at the reply again.
3. **Credit** an answer that the brief is the fact list, and unedited tool text can add claims they did not make. Those claims would then appear in all three drafts. **Missing** if they say the brief is only for show.
4. **Credit** an answer that they open a real page, compare the claim, and initial the log. **Do not credit** "ask the tool if it is sure", a "verified" stamp, or an address they did not open. There is no sample address in the course to memorise.
5. **Credit** a named human reading the final before send, file change, or spend, plus two of: passwords, codes, identity numbers, bank details, medical notes, real client records, private messages, or a real person's face or voice. **Missing** if the approver is the tool, or if they describe turning on auto-send.

A learner who misses one question can redo that lesson. Do not average the five into a score that says they are job-ready.

## Artifact rubric

Mark each row present, thin, or missing. "Thin" means the item exists but skips the rule in the note. Do not convert the rows into a percentage. Do not infer income, audience size, or time saved.

| Artifact | Present means | Thin or missing if |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plain-language card | In the learner's words: who, why, how, what it is not, and one goal | Copied from Sam or Nia with the sample left in as if it were theirs, or no goal |
| Prompt library | Several reusable prompts, aimed at 10, with a ban on invented facts | Only the tool's replies were saved, or prompts demand virality or guaranteed sales |
| Brief | One page, headings filled or marked "not stated", edited | A price, quote, or result the learner did not supply |
| Three drafts | Post, email, and description, each with an edit. Email not sent. Post not posted | A customer voice, a star rating, a discount, or a sent message |
| Source log | Titles and addresses copied from pages the learner opened, with their initials | Addresses from the chat tool that were never opened, or a "verified" stamp from the model. No credit for a fabricated address |
| Do-not-paste list | Applied to the brief and the three drafts | Secrets still in the brief, or a fake testimonial left in the post |
| Weekly map | Trigger, input, action, human approval, error handling. Not connected | Approval says "the tool", or a live send, purchase, or file move was turned on |
| Interest note | Optional. "Not yet" is complete | Treated as enrolment, a receipt, or an earnings promise |
| Knowledge check | All five answered in the learner's words | Answers pasted from this key, or graded by the chat tool |

Reviewer note, not for learners to chase: community membership and marketing consent are separate if a form exists. Absence of a form is not a fail. This draft has not been piloted, so a confusing row is a fault in the draft, not in the learner.
