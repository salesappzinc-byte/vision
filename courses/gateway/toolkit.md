## 2. Ten reusable prompts

Titles are suggestions. Edit the words so they match your goal. A prompt you do not understand does not count.

1. **Name the goal.** `Ask me up to five questions, one at a time, to help me name one small goal. Do not invent a business, a customer, a price, or a result. Wait for my answer. When I say stop, restate my goal in two sentences using only what I said, and mark gaps with "you did not say".`
2. **Build a prompt.** `Help me as [role]. Task: [draft / list / rewrite] this: [one sentence]. Context that is true: [facts]. Do not invent: [customers, prices, quotes, dates]. Format: [length and shape]. Example of tone: "[one line I wrote]."`
3. **Second pass.** `Review the reply above. List details you added that were not in my prompt. Delete them. Where you need a fact, write "you did not say" and one question. Do not send or post.`
4. **Interview for a brief.** `Interview me for a one-page brief. One question at a time. Cover who it is for, what they need, what I offer or what the project is, what I will not claim, tone, proof I actually have, tools I already use, and my next small step. Do not invent names, prices, quotes, or results. Then draft the page using only my answers.`
5. **Post.** `Using only the brief below, draft four to six lines in my voice. No prices, quotes, savings, or awards. End with "Draft for me to edit. Not posted." BRIEF: [paste].`
6. **Email, not sent.** `Using only the brief below, draft an email I will not send. Subject and under 120 words. No links. No prices. No one else's voice. End with "Practice copy. Do not send." BRIEF: [paste].`
7. **Description.** `Using only the brief below, write 6 to 10 lines: what it is, who it is for, what I will not promise. Missing facts become "not stated". No reviews and no results. BRIEF: [paste].`
8. **Summary from a page I opened.** `Summarise only the source text I paste. Do not add facts from memory. List each claim on its own line. Do not write "verified". Do not give me an address unless it is inside my pasted text. SOURCE TITLE: SOURCE ADDRESS: SOURCE TEXT:`
9. **Secrets and fake speakers.** `List possible secrets, invented quotes, savings claims, and any use of a real person's face or voice in the draft below. Then propose a shorter draft that removes them. End with "A person still needs to approve this. Do not send or post." DRAFT: [paste non-secret text].`
10. **Paper map only.** `Describe my weekly task as trigger, input, action, human approval, and error handling. Use only my notes. Do not tell me to connect email, files, or payments. Do not mention a price or a saving. End with "This is a paper map. Do not automate it." NOTES: [paste].`
