# Advanced course briefs

**Status:** Briefs only, 8 November 2026. Not written as full lessons. Not recorded. Not for sale. Do not list these as courses a learner can buy.

Each brief depends on the free gateway. None of them promise earnings, ad results, or a job. Purchasing automation stays the learner's own authorised use. Bypassing a site's controls is out of scope.

Shared sheets: [worksheets](../shared/worksheets.md).

## Coding and small app creation

**Depends on:** gateway prompts, and the website path's page plan if the app is a site.

**Modules:** what the app must do in one sentence; sample data only; a human reads the generated code before it is saved; what the learner will not connect (payments, private inboxes).

**Sample exercise:** ask a chat page to draft a checklist for a three-page app using only your brief. Delete any library it names unless you already use it. Do not run code you have not read.

**Capstone:** a written spec and a paper screen list. Not a deployed app.

**Integrations:** none required. If a learner later runs code, that is outside this brief and needs their own machine, not this repo.

**Consent and security:** no customer database. No pasted secrets. Generated code is untrusted until a person reads it.

**Priority:** after the website path, not before. Reason: the page plan is the smaller version of the same skill.

## Facebook and related ads

**Depends on:** the video path's honest clip. Creative first. Spend is the learner's money and is not required.

**Modules:** one offer in their words; a still or clip they have rights to; the difference between an ordinary ad, an organic post, and a political or social-issue ad; no promise of results.

**Sample exercise:** write three lines of ad text from the brief. Remove any result claim. Do not open an ad account.

**Capstone:** the text plus a note of which rule page the learner will read before any real ad. The course does not pick that page for every country.

**Integrations:** an ads account, later, is the learner's. This brief does not connect one.

**Consent and security:** no card details in the chat. No targeting of private lists pasted into the tool.

**Priority:** after the video path. Reason: the clip and the disclosure line should exist first.

## Bots and the stack

**Depends on:** gateway, plus the automation path's paper recipe.

**Modules:** what a bot is in one sentence; what a person still approves; a stack as names on paper (chat, storage, send) with send left off.

**Sample exercise:** draw three boxes and label what data would move. Circle the box that must stay off.

**Capstone:** the drawing plus a list of secrets that will never be pasted.

**Integrations:** none in the exercise.

**Consent and security:** a bot that messages people needs their consent. This brief does not send messages.

**Priority:** after automation. Reason: the paper recipe is the safer version.

## Calls, email, SMS, and WhatsApp

**Depends on:** gateway do-not-paste list and the automation approval line.

**Modules:** a message the learner would actually send; consent before a commercial message; country rules are not taught as one global law; the exercise stops before send.

**Sample exercise:** draft one message to "a friend who agreed on paper". Mark it "not sent". Delete any line that messages a list.

**Capstone:** the draft plus the name of the person who would approve a real send later.

**Integrations:** a phone, email, or WhatsApp account is the learner's. Do not connect it here.

**Consent and security:** no bulk send. No bought lists. No impersonation.

**Priority:** after automation. Reason: sending is the dangerous step the paper map avoids.

## Authorised purchasing

**Depends on:** gateway, and a written statement of which shop account is the learner's.

**Modules:** what may be bought only on an account the learner owns and is allowed to use; reading the shop's terms; stopping when a control or a check appears; no bypass, no shared passwords, no bot that pretends to be a different user.

**Sample exercise:** write the steps to buy one sample item as a person, on paper, including "stop if the site asks for a code I did not expect". Do not complete a purchase.

**Capstone:** the paper steps and a line: "I will not evade a check."

**Integrations:** a shop account, later, is the learner's. This brief does not log in.

**Consent and security:** only the account holder. No stored card in the chat. No script that clicks through warnings.

**Priority:** last. Reason: the harm of a mistake is a real charge, and the other paths do not need it.

File organisation remains lesson 4 of the automation path unless a later review shows learners cannot finish that lesson. No such evidence exists yet.
