# Paid path draft: a small website

**Status:** Written draft, 8 November 2026. Not recorded. Not for sale.

**Audience:** someone who wants a few public pages and can already write a brief. No coding is required.

**Prerequisite:** free gateway brief.

**Tool cost:** a site builder, domain, or host, if you buy one, is your cost. This draft does not require a purchase. A preview on a free tool is enough. Record the tool on the [shared sheet](../../shared/worksheets.md).

**Lessons:** [01 Plan the pages](01-plan-the-pages.md) is script-ready. [Lessons 2 to 5](lessons.md) are notes, not recordings.

**Project:** a page plan plus either a preview address you opened yourself or a paper mock. No traffic claim.

**Rubric:** pages are listed; words match the brief; a privacy note is drafted; images have a text alternative; you did not publish a fake review.

**Quiz:** end of [lessons.md](lessons.md).
