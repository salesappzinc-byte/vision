# Paid path draft: honest product video

**Status:** Written draft, 8 November 2026. Not recorded. Not for sale. Not a finished course.

People who finished the free gateway can practise a short product or creator clip without pretending a customer spoke. A later course may be sold only after the owner sets a price. This folder does not do that.

**Audience:** a solo business, creator, or marketer with one real offer, or a personal project they can show. Beginners who can already edit a draft.

**Prerequisite:** the free gateway brief and the do-not-paste list.

**Tool cost:** a video tool, if you use one, is your cost. It is not included here. Write the name and version on the [shared tool line](../../shared/worksheets.md). No price is stated in this draft.

**Lessons:** [01 Honest clip](01-honest-clip.md) is script-ready. [Lessons 2 to 5](lessons.md) are teaching notes, not recordings.

**Project:** a short clip pack for one labelled item: script, what is real versus generated, rights line, and a disclosure line. Nothing is posted.

**Rubric:** the pack exists; no fake customer; no earnings or view claim; tool version is written; a person approved it; it was not posted.

**Quiz:** see the end of [lessons.md](lessons.md).
