# Lesson 1. A short clip that tells the truth

**Status:** Script-ready draft. Not recorded. Not for sale.

**Objective:** Write a 20 to 40 second script for one item you can show, with every invented detail marked and removed.

**You need:** your gateway brief, paper, and a browser chat you are allowed to use. A video tool is optional and not required to finish this lesson.

## Teach

A product clip fails when it sounds like a stranger loved the thing. You do not have that stranger. You have an object, a label, and words you can stand behind.

Work in four beats. What the item is. Who it is for. One action the viewer can see. What you will not claim. If a line needs a customer, a saving, or a view count, it is out.

Sample, labelled fictional: a tin on a desk, called Sample Tin. It is not a brand. The script may say "This tin holds tea" only if that is what you decided. It may not say "customers switched" or "this ad made sales".

Nia's book swap, if you are not selling anything: the "item" is the swap itself. No date, no library yes, no neighbour quote.

## Exercise

1. Film nothing yet. Write four beats on paper.
2. Paste only those beats into a browser chat with the prompt below.
3. On the [edit log](../../shared/worksheets.md), delete every extra claim.
4. Read the script aloud. If you would not say it on camera, cut it.

## Prompt

```
Turn the beats below into a spoken script of 20 to 40 seconds.
Use only these beats. Do not add a customer, a price, a saving, or a view count.
Mark any missing fact as "not stated".
End with: "Practice script. Do not post."

BEATS:
[paste your four beats]
```

## Artifact

`script.txt` in your notes, plus one edit-log row. Posted? No.

## Check

- I can point to a sentence I removed.
- No person's face or voice is copied.
- I did not open a checkout.
