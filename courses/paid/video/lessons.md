# Video path lessons 2 to 5

**Status:** Teaching notes, 8 November 2026. Not scripted as full narration. Not recorded. Not for sale.

Lesson 1 is [01-honest-clip.md](01-honest-clip.md). Use the [shared worksheets](../../shared/worksheets.md).

Official product pages change. On 8 November 2026, ByteDance's Seedance 2.5 page described clips up to 30 seconds with two extensions. That is their claim about their model, not a rule for your clip, and not a tool this course requires. Write whatever length your script needs, inside 20 to 40 seconds for the exercise.

## Lesson 2. What the camera actually saw

**Teach:** Split the clip into shots you could film and shots a tool would invent. A hand holding Sample Tin can be filmed. A crowd cheering cannot, unless you filmed a crowd.

**Exercise:** Make a two-column list, Real and Generated. One row minimum each, or "none" if you will not generate.

**Prompt:** `List shots for this script. Do not add people I did not name. Label each shot real or generated. SCRIPT: [paste]`

**Artifact:** the two-column list.

**Check:** every generated shot has a reason you can say aloud.

## Lesson 3. Voice, captions, and a real face

**Teach:** Your voice, spoken by you, needs no extra permission from yourself. Another person's face or voice needs their yes. A cloned voice is out for this exercise. Captions are words on screen so the clip can be read without sound.

**Exercise:** Write three caption lines that match the script. Note "my voice" or "no voice yet".

**Prompt:** `Write captions for this script. Do not change the facts. SCRIPT: [paste]`

**Artifact:** caption lines plus the rights row on the shared sheet.

**Check:** captions do not add a claim the script lacks.

## Lesson 4. No customer who is not there

**Teach:** UGC-style means the clip looks like a person showing a thing. It does not mean you invent that person. If you are in the frame, say so. If a tool draws a presenter, the caption must say the presenter is generated.

**Exercise:** Add one disclosure line: "I filmed this" or "The presenter is generated. No customer is speaking."

**Prompt:** `Add one disclosure sentence to this script. Do not add a testimonial. SCRIPT: [paste]`

**Artifact:** script plus disclosure line.

**Check:** a stranger can tell who is speaking.

## Lesson 5. Pack and stop

**Teach:** The pack is script, shot list, captions, disclosure, tool name and version, and your approval. Posting is a later choice, not this lesson.

**Exercise:** Assemble the pack. Fill the tool line. Sign the human-approval line. Do not upload the clip anywhere.

**Artifact:** the pack.

**Check:** rubric on the [path index](index.md). Nothing was posted.

## Quiz

1. What must you remove if the tool adds a customer quote?
2. Where do you record the video tool's name and version?
3. What sentence tells a viewer the presenter is generated?

**Key:** 1. The quote, and any result that came with it. 2. On the shared tool line, copied from the tool's screen. 3. A plain disclosure, not a hidden credit. Accept equivalent words.
