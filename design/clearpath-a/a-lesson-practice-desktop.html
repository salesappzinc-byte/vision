What people mean by “AI”

Plain language first: when people say “AI” they usually mean software that predicts or generates useful text, images, or actions—not a single robot with one personality.

Video placeholder — no fabricated lesson footage

In this lesson

You will separate hype from everyday use: assistants that draft and summarize, tools that suggest next steps, and automation that follows rules you set. None of this requires you to “trust the magic.” You will name what the tool is doing in words you could explain to a friend.

AI is not one thing. It is a set of techniques people apply to specific jobs—writing help, research help, image help, workflow help. Your job is to match the job to the tool, not the other way around.

On-site practice

Write in plain English: one task you already do weekly where a draft-and-edit assistant might save you time. Do not paste private data. This stays in your toolkit on Clearpath.
